The Foundry Operating Model

How Ops Foundry converts client business backlogs into structured workflows, managed pods, and verified deliverables.

* Proposed execution framework subject to final operational review.

1

Intake & Scoping

Sprint Week 01

We audit your operational bottlenecks, document existing processes, and define measurable deliverables. Rather than ambiguous consulting reports, this stage produces an explicit execution agreement detailing sprint objectives, deliverables, and acceptance criteria.

Key Output: Operational Sprint Brief & Deliverables Acceptance Checklist.
2

Workflow Design

Sprint Week 02

Work cannot scale on tribal knowledge alone. In this stage, we author Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), configure the AI prompt templates and automated pipelines, and assemble the tooling environment for human operators.

Key Output: Documented SOPs, QA Checkpoints, and Configured AI Toolchains.
3

Pod Deployment

Sprint Weeks 02–03

A dedicated operational pod takes ownership of daily task execution. Operators work within established SOPs, accelerating output with configured AI tools while conducting strict quality reviews at every milestone.

Key Output: Active task execution, sprint updates, and weekly milestone demos.
4

Review & Handover

Sprint Week 04

Deliverables are formally verified against the initial acceptance criteria. Complete operational documentation and codebase/platform access are handed over to your internal team, or transitioned to recurring maintenance.

Key Output: Final Deliverable Package, System Handover, and Operational Documentation.
