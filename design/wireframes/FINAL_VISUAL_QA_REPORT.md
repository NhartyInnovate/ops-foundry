## 4. Comprehensive Section-by-Section Evaluations

### 4.1 Corporate Homepage Audit
* **Header & Navigation:** Unobtrusive, clean frosted glass header (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/95`). The monogram logo with orange focal dot feels established. Hover-activated mega-dropdown provides direct access to individual services without visual clutter.
* **Hero Section:** The headline *"Your business has work to do. We help you get it done."* commands immediate focus (`leading-[1.12]` tight leading). The replacement of software-coding imagery with authentic operations team coordination photography creates immediate B2B credibility. The photo overlay caption has been softened into a discreet frosted badge.
* **Why Ops Foundry Exists:** Editorial layout on crisp white ground. Contradicts software hype with grounded business realities (software alone doesn't execute; unmanaged freelancers lack consistency; Ops Foundry manages the execution).
* **The Three Operating Pillars:** Clean 3-column card grid explaining *Managed Teams*, *Structured Workflows*, and *AI-Enabled Tools*. All fake `PILLAR 01` tags have been removed.
* **Services Section:** Editorial rows for *Get Online*, *Get Visible*, and *Get Customers*. All eyebrow tags have been eliminated. Single clear contextual link per service.
* **How We Work:** High-contrast industrial black (`#111215`) background. 4-phase execution framework is clearly labeled with an explicit disclaimer: `* Proposed execution framework subject to final operational review`.
* **Academy Bridge:** Warm off-white container connecting corporate execution with the Academy talent pipeline.
* **Pilot Intake Form:** Clean B2B inquiry form. Free of unrealistic SLA promises (e.g. 24h guarantee), replacing them with direct review commitments.

### 4.2 Academy Homepage Audit
* **Tone & Persona:** Warm, encouraging, and academically grounded without mimicking a SaaS LMS.
* **Hero Section:** Headline *"Build the skills to do real business work."* paired with active workshop collaboration photography.
* **Methodology:** Replaces theoretical claims with practical workplace simulations.
* **Programmes Presentation:** Neutral placeholders: **Course One**, **Course Two**, and **Course Three**. No unconfirmed course names or durations are presented as final.
* **How Learning Works:** Dark scholastic slate (`#1E232E`) container detailing the 3 practical learning pillars.
* **Tuition & Scholarships:** Fully transparent with explicit `[To be confirmed]` placeholders.
* **Career Readiness:** Avoids unverified employment guarantees; focuses strictly on practical demonstrable competencies.

### 4.3 Supporting Pages Audit
* **Corporate Services (`corporate-services.html`):** The transition from repetitive rounded cards to full-width editorial sections with subtle dividers and documentary photography gives the page an executive, consultative feel.
* **Corporate Service Detail (`corporate-service-detail.html`):** Interactive tab selector operates smoothly; scope and dependencies matrices are structured clearly.
* **Academy Programmes (`academy-programmes.html`):** Structured directory layout with distinct course rows, authentic photography, and provisional metadata tags.
* **Academy Admissions (`academy-admissions.html`):** 3-stage process (*Application*, *Profile Review*, *Enrollment*) paired with an interactive course selection form.
* **Academy Tuition (`academy-tuition.html`):** Clear comparison cards, scholarship terms, and a dedicated sponsor engagement section.

## 6. Content & Brand Audit Findings

* **Eyebrow Headings:** 100% eliminated across all files (`SERVICE 01..03`, `COURSE ONE..THREE`, `01 / INTAKE`, etc. have been completely removed).
* **Typography Consistency:** Strictly standardized on `Manrope` (Display) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body). Monospace typography as a decorative device has been eradicated.
* **Technical Jargon & SaaS Tropes:** Removed fake system schematics, glowing pulse dots, and artificial dashboard mockups.
* **Unsupported Business Claims:** 
  * No invented customer logos or fabricated testimonials.
  * No 24-hour response guarantees.
  * No automatic employment guarantees for Academy students.
  * No unapproved technical services claimed as standard deliverables.
* **Link Validation:** All navigation links, cross-domain pills (`Ops Foundry Academy ↗` and `Ops Foundry Corporate ↗`), and query parameters resolve properly within the prototype.
